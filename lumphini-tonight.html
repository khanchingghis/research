03 · The sports

What is allowed, bout by bout

Read the colored tag on the fight. Then use that column. The glove is the easiest tell from your seat: Muay Thai and MMA wear tiny open-finger 4 oz gloves. Kickboxing wears bigger closed 8 oz gloves.

Muay Thai

4 oz gloves · 3 × 3 minutes · 1 minute rest

  • Punches Yes
  • Kicks Yes
  • Knees Yes
  • Elbows Yes
  • Clinch and sweeps Yes
  • Ground fighting No

Kickboxing

8 oz gloves · 3 × 3 minutes · 1 minute rest

  • Punches Yes
  • Kicks Yes
  • Knees Yes
  • Elbows Foul
  • Long clinch Broken up
  • Ground fighting No

MMA

4 oz gloves · 3 × 5 minutes · 1 minute rest

  • Punches Yes
  • Kicks Yes
  • Knees and elbows Yes
  • Takedowns Yes
  • Ground striking Yes
  • Submissions A finish

A fight can end by knockout, by the referee or a corner stopping it, by three knockdowns in one round, or by four knockdowns in the bout. In the two MMA fights, a tap of the hand, or a fighter caught in a choke or a joint lock, is the same kind of ending. Three knockdowns do not apply there. If the striking fight goes the distance, three judges score every round. If an MMA fight goes the distance, those judges pick a winner of the whole bout, using damage, near-finishes, control, and takedowns.

Eight limbs

Two fists, two elbows, two knees, two shins. Boxing is the fists alone. Kickboxing adds the kicks and the knees, then stops. Muay Thai is the full set. A legal throw in Muay Thai is a sweep or a trip, not a wrestling slam.

What each limb is for

TeepA push kick to the hip or stomach. It buys space. It scores when it moves someone, not when it merely looks tidy.
KickShin to thigh, ribs, or head. A limp step afterward is damage, even with no knockdown.
ClinchBoth hands tied up at the neck, knees coming up the middle. If nothing lands, the referee breaks it.
ElbowThe Lumpinee weapon. One clean one can open a cut and end the fight. Illegal in both kickboxing bouts tonight.

Muay Thai bouts open with a thank-you to the teacher. Kickboxing and MMA do not. If they kneel, you are watching Muay Thai. If they only touch gloves, you are not.

04 · How to watch a round

Give the winner 10. Give the other fighter 9, or 8.

Striking rounds use the 10-point must system. Add the rounds up. A 10–8 round is the one you will still be describing in the taxi.

10–10

Almost even. Judges hate writing this. If you cannot separate them, you are allowed to.

10–9

The ordinary round. Somebody landed the cleaner, harder shots. That person gets the 10.

10–8

A knockdown, or a round that was not close. Two knockdowns can make it 10–7. The fight may already be over.

Order of importance on a striking scorecard: knockdowns, damage, clean shots landed, then who was making the fight happen. In the two MMA bouts, ignore this ticket. Those judges pick a winner of the whole fight, not a 10–9 for each round.

The band tells you the round

The sarama sits ringside: a pi chawa oboe, a pair of drums, and small ching cymbals. The tune is the same idea all night. The speed is the information.

Round 1
Long, careful, a lot of teeps.
Round 2
Harder kicks. Someone picks a side.
Round 3
The sprint. This is the round that swings a close fight.

ไหว้ครู · the wai khru

Only before Muay Thai. It takes about a minute. The dance is a thank-you, performed for the teacher who taught the fighter.

  1. 1He walks the rope and seals the ring, usually counterclockwise, touching the corners.
  2. 2Three bows in the center, for his teacher and the teachers before that one.
  3. 3The ram muay, a short dance that belongs to his camp. You do not need to decode it.
  4. 4His trainer lifts off the mongkon, the blessed headband. Nobody else handles it. The prajioud armbands stay on.

During the bows

Stay in your seat and keep your voice down until the mongkon comes off. If an anthem plays at the start of the night, stand. After the bell, shout all you want.

Between rounds

You will hear numbers yelled in Thai. That is fans pricing bets with each other. It is part of the room. One minute, then the band starts again.

Corners

Red and blue are just the two corners. The color does not tell you who is favored, and it does not tell you who deserves the cheer.

The two MMA fights

A takedown is the sport, not a stall. Watch the hips and the hands. A trapped arm or a neck is how those fights end before the clock does.
